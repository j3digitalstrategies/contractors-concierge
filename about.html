About The Contractor's Concierge

Beautiful spaces. Building lasting relationships.

A More Human Way to Build

The Contractor's Concierge is designed to make the home-building and renovation experience feel more manageable, personal, and connected. We believe the process should feel organized and supported — not overwhelming.

I am an independent ADU, home addition, and remodel consultant, concierge, and liaison for homeowners (I do not charge you for this service), as well as professionals in the trades. I am also the project coordinator for several vetted, licensed, and insured general contractors, a LEED-accredited architectural firm, civil engineers (who are also drainage and water intrusion experts), soil, geo, and structural engineers handling projects across the Berkeley, Oakland, San Francisco, and Marin hills, an independent certified home inspector specializing in retro permitting, a mold and water damage remediation contractor, landscape architects, contractors, and designers, local custom cabinet and vanity makers, local custom ironworkers, master finish carpenters, and fantastic interior designers skilled in both residential and commercial projects—all independent from each other.

I create design-build teams where you are able to choose the best fit for your project. When the time comes for bidding, you will have several vetted contractors to choose from, all of whom have worked closely with the architects, designers, and engineers.

Over the past 10 years, I've cultivated trusted general contractors, architects, masons and tilers, landscape designers and contractors, electricians, engineers, designers, and other trades professionals for any type of home improvement project or ADU, big or small. I help you get the best value for your dollar while working within your budget, and my primary function is to make sure that it all flows seamlessly!
